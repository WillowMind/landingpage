Four ways in, from free self-care to a licensed counsellor. Move through them in any order, and share only what you want to.

How it works

Get started without the pressure.

Three steps take you from a short questionnaire to your first session. You stay in control at every stage.

Join waitlist
  1. 01

    Answer a few questions

    A short questionnaire about what you’re going through. It isn’t a diagnosis. We use it to find the right counsellor for you.

  2. 02

    Get matched

    We match you with a counsellor by language, what you’re dealing with, your budget and the times that suit you. You choose from the suggestions.

  3. 03

    Talk and keep going

    Pick video, voice or chat, anonymous or with your name. Check in with your counsellor at your own pace.

Questions people ask.

It’s counselling for everyday difficulties like stress, anxiety, low mood, relationships and grief. It isn’t for emergencies or for anything that needs medication. If your situation needs that kind of care, your counsellor will tell you and help you reach it.

Yes. You can use a nickname and leave out your name. The one limit is emergencies. We can’t send help to someone we can’t identify, so we show helplines and stay in the chat with you.

Sinhala, Tamil and English. You pick your language when you’re matched, and we only show counsellors who speak it.

You pay per session in rupees, with a local card or payment method. The price depends on the counsellor you choose. Self-care tools are free.

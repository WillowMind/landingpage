A gentle path forward

Four ways in, from free self-care to a licensed counsellor. Move through them in any order, and share only what you want to. Empowering your mental well-being through accessible support, personalised care, and a healthier approach to everyday life.

Explore

Take a short check-in and explore music, movies and art that match how you feel. No pressure and you choose how much to share.

Understand

See what your answers suggest, learn about what you're experiencing in plain language and find out what kind of support might help.

Connect

Talk to a trained counsellor when you're ready, and be matched with a licensed therapist if you need more. You decide what gets shared.

Thrive

Keep building small habits with coping tools, creative outlets and support that stays with you at your own pace.

How it works

Get started without the pressure.

Three steps take you from a short questionnaire to your first session. You stay in control at every stage.

Join waitlist Join waitlist
01

Answer a few questionsAssessment

A short questionnaire about what you’re going through. It isn’t a diagnosis. We use it to find the right counsellor for you.

02

Get matched

We match you with a counsellor by language, what you’re dealing with, your budget and the times that suit you. You choose from the suggestions.

03

Talk and keep going

Pick video, voice or chat, anonymous or with your name. Check in with your counsellor at your own pace.

FAQ

Questions people ask. Your questions answered.

Everything you need to know before you start. Still have questions? Join the waitlist and we’ll get back to you.

It’s counselling for everyday difficulties like stress, anxiety, low mood, relationships and grief. It isn’t for emergencies or for anything that needs medication. If your situation needs that kind of care, your counsellor will tell you and help you reach it.

Yes. You can use a nickname and leave out your name. The one limit is emergencies. We can’t send help to someone we can’t identify, so we show helplines and stay in the chat with you.

Sinhala, Tamil and English. You pick your language when you’re matched, and we only show counsellors who speak it.

You pay per session in rupees, with a local card or payment method. The price depends on the counsellor you choose. Self-care tools are free.

The questions help us find a counsellor who suits you and spot anyone who needs more support than we can give. They aren’t a diagnosis. You can skip most of them. The safety questions are always asked, because they let us look after you.
